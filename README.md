# Statistics class laboratories
Repository of solutions to my **Biophysics** laboratories on the **5th semester** of studying Mathematics at Gdańsk University of Technology

## The laboratory outline
1. Determination of the VO₂max Oxygen Uptake Capacity
2. Determination of Diffusion Potential
3. Study of Optical Systems Properties
4. Determination of the Human Visual Field
5. Acoustic Spatial Orientation
6. Measurement of the Temporal Resolving Power of the Human Eye
7. Determination of the Hearing Threshold and Frequency Discrimination Threshold
8. Measurement of the Refractive Index and Determination of Solution Concentrations Using an Abbe Refractometer
